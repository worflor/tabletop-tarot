Tabletop Tarot — one ZIP, two vanilla pack locations

Keep this ZIP intact. It serves as both the datapack and resource pack.

SINGLEPLAYER
1. Copy this ZIP into your world's datapacks folder.
2. Copy the same ZIP into Minecraft's resourcepacks folder, then enable it.
3. Reload or reopen the world. No mod or plugin is required.

SERVER
Install this ZIP in the world's datapacks folder. Host the same unchanged ZIP
at a direct download URL and set resource-pack and resource-pack-sha1 in
server.properties. To calculate this ZIP's SHA-1 in PowerShell, run:
Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA1 ./Tabletop-Tarot-1.1.0.zip
Use the hash shown, without spaces. GitHub release notes list the published hash.
Players can then accept Minecraft's normal server resource-pack prompt.
On older releases, keep Tabletop Tarot above other packs that replace item
models (for example 3D Default). Ordinary texture replacements remain usable.
If your server already offers resources, combine the packs during server setup;
do not replace the existing server pack without preserving it.

UPGRADING AND REMOVAL
Back up your world before upgrading. Replace the old ZIP in both pack locations,
then reopen the world or reload the server. Update the server pack URL and hash
as needed. Existing signed guides keep their original pages.
Before removing the pack, pick up any cards you want to keep while it is still
active. Remove the ZIP from both pack locations and restart. Stored card items
remain in the world, but handling actions and custom appearance require the
pack. Cards left on tables retain their display entities and may need cleanup.

PLAY
Craft eight paper around the matching centre ingredient:
- Rider: ink sac
- Numbers: lapis lazuli
The normal recipe book learns a deck when you have its ingredients.
Deck in hand: use draws the top card; sneak-use shuffles.
With a card offhand, sneak-use on a deck puts that card on top instead.
Card in hand: use places it face-down on a clear flat surface (blocks, slabs,
carpets, closed trapdoors, or upside-down stairs). Aim at the top face; sides
and undersides do not place cards. Sneak-use avoids opening a workstation.
On a placed card, use turns it over, sneak-use rotates it, and hit picks it up;
hitting while holding a deck puts it straight back on top (sneak-hit gathers a spread).
Q remains an ordinary item drop.
Keep the offhand empty for ordinary use, except when returning a card.
Vanilla blocks, usable offhand items and ridden pigs can take priority over use.
The dependable alternative is /trigger tarot.action set N:
1 draws, 2 shuffles, 3 returns the offhand card, 4 places the held card, 5 deals face-down.
These commands also work while mounted and do not require operator permissions.
Existing paper-based cards retain their notes and contents. Drawing, returning
or placing one upgrades its carrier; hold a band offhand to place, or use
/trigger tarot.action set 4. Until handled, older items still count as paper
in crafting, so keep untouched older cards out of paper recipes.
New cards and bands also have vanilla tool-repair eligibility removed. Successful
handling upgrades older carriers; keep untouched earlier items out of tool-repair
crafting. Custom durability-capacity overrides are saved in their card data.

Optional guides: one plain book with one amethyst shard makes Steve's guide;
add a feather for Alex's (better) guide. Steve's has 109 linked pages including
a P.S.; Alex's has 108. Both cover the same controls, all 78 tarot cards and
the 12 Numbers, with different personal Minecraft readings.
Read either in your hand or share it on a lectern. Existing signed guides keep
their pages. These imagined journals are part of the pack's own lore.
Craft fresh guides for the new covers and wording. Their matte 3D item models
need the resource pack; the reading screen and lectern book use vanilla rendering.

Only your marked cards and bands respond. There is no welcome screen,
automatic item grant, mandatory tutorial, or enforced reading/game.
Vanilla fire, lava, despawn and inventory-space rules still apply.

SUPPORTED JAVA RELEASES
1.20.6, 1.21.1, 1.21.3, 1.21.4, 1.21.5, 1.21.6, 1.21.8, 1.21.10, 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.3.
Other releases and Bedrock are not covered.
